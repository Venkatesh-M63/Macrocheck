How MacroCheck works

MacroCheck estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula — the same equation used by most dietitians and fitness apps — then multiplies it by your activity level to get your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). From there, it adjusts your calorie target based on your goal, and splits your macros (protein, carbs, fat) so you know exactly what to eat, not just how much.

Example calculation

A 21-year-old male, 170 cm, 65 kg, who exercises 3–5 days a week and wants to build muscle: his BMR is around 1,590 kcal. Multiplied by a moderate activity factor of 1.55, his TDEE is roughly 2,465 kcal. Adding a lean surplus for muscle gain brings his target to about 2,765 kcal/day, with protein set at around 130 g to support training.

Important notes

  • These numbers are estimates — your actual needs can vary by ±10–15% based on genetics, sleep, stress and medical conditions.
  • Protein targets are set higher during a calorie deficit to help protect muscle while losing fat.
  • Re-calculate every few weeks as your weight changes, especially during active fat loss or muscle building phases.

Frequently asked questions

What is MacroCheck?

MacroCheck is a free online calculator that tells you your daily calorie and protein needs based on your age, height, weight, activity level and goal.

How accurate is this calorie calculator?

It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, considered one of the most accurate formulas for estimating calorie needs in the general population. It's a strong starting point, though individual results vary.

How much protein do I need per day?

Most active adults need roughly 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of bodyweight, depending on their goal. MacroCheck calculates this automatically based on your inputs.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, completely free, with no signup or payment required.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, MacroCheck is built mobile-first and works smoothly on any smartphone, tablet or laptop.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use the full calculator instantly without logging in or signing up.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations happen instantly in your browser — nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

Can I use this if I'm vegetarian?

Yes — the protein target applies regardless of diet. Use the Indian food table for vegetarian-friendly sources like paneer, dal, curd and rajma.
